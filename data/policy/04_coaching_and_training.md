# Coaching and training time (WFM-POL-04)

Applies to all scheduled off-phone development time. Owner: Quality and Training.
Version 2026-09.

## TRN-01 Coaching may be cancelled intraday
One-to-one coaching and optional training sessions may be cancelled on the day to put
agents back on the phones. Cancelled sessions carry a soft cost of Rs 150 per session hour
because development time is lost.

## TRN-02 Compliance training is mandatory
Compliance training (anti-money-laundering, data protection, fraud awareness) is a
regulatory requirement and can never be cancelled or shortened intraday.

## TRN-03 Rescheduling
Every cancelled coaching session must be rescheduled within 5 working days. Repeatedly
cancelling the same agent's coaching should be avoided.
