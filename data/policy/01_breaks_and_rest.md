# Breaks and rest periods (WFM-POL-01)

Applies to every agent on both queues. Owner: Workforce Management. Version 2026-09.

## BRK-01 Delaying a break during a spike
A scheduled tea break or lunch may be moved later by at most 45 minutes to cover an
intraday spike. A break is moved, never cancelled: every agent still takes every break
they were scheduled for, inside their shift.

## BRK-02 Continuous work limit
No agent may work more than 5 hours continuously without a rest interval. When delaying a
break, check that the delay does not push the agent past 5 hours of continuous work since
their shift start or their previous break.

## BRK-03 Breaks already in progress
A break that has already started cannot be interrupted or shortened. Only breaks that have
not yet begun may be moved.

## BRK-04 Fairness of break moves
When breaks must move, spread the delay across the team rather than repeatedly delaying the
same agents. A single agent should not have more than one break delayed in the same day.
