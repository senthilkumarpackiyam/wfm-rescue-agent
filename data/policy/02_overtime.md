# Overtime (WFM-POL-02)

Applies to every agent. Owner: Workforce Management with HR. Version 2026-09.

## OT-01 Overtime is voluntary
Overtime is offered, never ordered. Agents may decline without consequence. Planning
assumes about 40% of agents offered overtime accept it.

## OT-02 Overtime hours per agent
An agent may work at most 2 hours of overtime in a day. A single overtime offer must not
ask any agent for more than 2 hours.

## OT-03 Total daily working hours
Including overtime, no agent may work more than 10 hours in a day.

## OT-04 Daily overtime budget
Intraday overtime for the whole centre is capped at Rs 15,000 per day unless the duty
manager approves more in writing. The supervisor may lower the cap for the day (for example
"no overtime budget today"), in which case no overtime may be offered. Overtime slots are booked
first come, first served: once the budget is fully booked, further volunteers are thanked and
declined, so actual spend never exceeds the cap.

## OT-05 Overtime rate
Overtime is paid at double the normal hourly rate: Rs 360 per hour against the standard
Rs 180 per hour.

## OT-06 Lead time
Agents need about 30 minutes to accept an offer and log in. Overtime cannot help any
interval that starts less than 30 minutes after the offer is approved.
