# Skills and intraday moves between queues (WFM-POL-03)

Applies to the Payments & UPI and Accounts & General queues. Owner: Workforce Management.
Version 2026-09.

## MOV-01 Only cross-skilled agents can move
Only agents trained and certified on both queues may be moved to the other queue. About a
quarter of agents hold both skills.

## MOV-02 Maximum number of agents moved
No more than 30% of the agents on the donor queue may be moved away at the same time.

## MOV-03 Protecting the donor queue
A move is allowed only if the donor queue is still predicted to answer at least 70% of its
calls within 20 seconds while the move lasts. Moving agents must not create a second
crisis on the other queue.

## MOV-04 Duration and return
Every move must have an end time. Moved agents return to their home queue at that time,
or earlier if the donor queue's service level falls below 70%.
